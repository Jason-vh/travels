01 — The Five Villages

The coast at a glance

I
Monterosso al Mare

The northernmost village and the largest. The only real beach. Lemon groves, sandy coves, and a relaxed town feel. A good base.

II
Vernazza

Arguably the most beautiful. A natural harbour, a medieval watchtower, and a piazza that spills directly into the sea.

III
Corniglia

The only village not at sea level. Perched high on a promontory, reached by 382 steps. Quieter. Better views.

IV
Manarola

Colourful stacked houses above a rocky inlet. Famous for its wine, its sunsets, and the Via dell'Amore path south to Riomaggiore.

V
Riomaggiore

The southernmost village. A steep main street, cliffside swimming spots, and the best access to the wilder coastal trails.

02 — Day by Day

Four days on the cliffs

0
Friday, May 22
Arrival & First Light

A travel day — get in, get settled, get a glass of local white wine in hand. No agenda beyond arrival and orientation. Dinner together as the sun drops into the sea.

  • Afternoon
    Arrive in Monterosso or La Spezia
    Train connections from Milan Centrale or Genova Piazza Principe. The Cinque Terre line runs frequently.
  • Evening
    Check in & settle
    Drop bags, get orientated, walk the main street of your base village.
  • Night
    Group dinner — local trattoria
    Focaccia, anchovies, fresh pasta, Sciacchetrà wine. The whole group together. Keep it unhurried.
1
Saturday, May 23
Northern Trail & the Three Villages

The classic northern stretch. Hike from Monterosso through Vernazza to Corniglia along the Sentiero Azzurro (Blue Trail). Take it slowly. Swim where you can. Eat when hungry.

  • Morning
    Monterosso — early walk & coffee
    Espresso and cornetto before the crowds. Walk the old town before heading to the trailhead.
  • Mid-morning
    Hike: Monterosso to Vernazza
    Approx. 3.8 km, 1.5–2 hrs. Moderate trail with commanding sea views. Cinque Terre Card required (Trail 2).
  • Midday
    Vernazza — lunch & swim
    Lunch in the piazza. Jump in the harbour if the water is calling. Explore the castle tower.
  • Afternoon
    Hike: Vernazza to Corniglia
    Approx. 4.1 km, 1.5–2 hrs. Quieter trail, more wooded. Finish with the 382-step staircase into the village.
  • Late afternoon
    Corniglia — gelato & views
    The reward. Drinks on a terrace above everything. Take the train back to base when ready.
  • Evening
    Rest & dinner back at base
    Legs will be tired. Something simple and good.
2
Sunday, May 24
Southern Villages & the Sea

A slower day. Train south to Riomaggiore in the morning, work back north through Manarola. More swimming, less hiking. Watch the light change over Manarola in the late afternoon — it is one of the best views in Italy.

  • Morning
    Train to Riomaggiore
    Three minutes from Manarola. Walk the main street, find a cliffside rock and swim. The water is clearest early.
  • Late morning
    Coastal swimming at Riomaggiore
    Rocky entry points south of the village. Bring water shoes. Exceptionally clear Ligurian water in May.
  • Midday
    Walk or train to Manarola
    Via dell'Amore (the Love Path) connects the two — check trail status before going, as it re-opened after restoration in 2024.
  • Afternoon
    Manarola — lunch, wine, and a swim
    Sciacchetrà, the local sweet wine, is best here. The swimming platform below the village is a favourite spot.
  • Late afternoon
    The Manarola viewpoint at golden hour
    Head to the eastern hill above the village. The stacked coloured houses at sunset is the photograph everyone comes for.
  • Evening
    Last proper group dinner
    The final full night. Make it count — book ahead if possible.
3
Monday, May 25
Slow Morning & Departure

No rush. Check-out is a ritual, not a race. A last coffee, a last look at the sea, then the train north.

  • Morning
    Final breakfast in the village
    Sit with coffee for longer than you need to. Walk to the water one last time.
  • Late morning
    Check out & luggage storage
    Most accommodation will hold bags until early afternoon. Use the time for a final village wander.
  • Midday
    Depart via La Spezia or Levanto
    Frequent trains connect north to Genova and Milan, and south toward Pisa and Florence.

04 — Practical

What to know before you go

The Cinque Terre Card
  • Required for hiking — all Sentiero Azzurro trails require a valid card. Buy at any village train station or online.
  • Trekking Card (without train): approx. €7.50/day per person. Covers all open trails within the National Park.
  • Cinque Terre Card + Train: approx. €18.20 / 2-day, covers unlimited trains between villages plus all trails.
  • Trail conditions vary seasonally — check parconazionale5terre.it before each day's hike.
Getting Around
  • The Trenitalia regional line connects all five villages. Trains run every 15–30 minutes; the journey end-to-end is under 15 minutes.
  • Cars are not useful inside the villages. Parking outside is limited and expensive. Arrive by train.
  • Ferries run between villages (seasonal, weather-dependent) and offer a different perspective on the coast. Worth doing once.
  • The nearest major hub is La Spezia Centrale — good connections south (Pisa, Florence) and north (Genova, Milan).
Weather in May
  • Ideal conditions: late May is warm (19–24°C), mostly sunny, and before peak summer heat and crowds.
  • Sea temperature sits around 18–20°C in late May — brisk but entirely swimmable, particularly after hiking.
  • Afternoon thunderstorms are possible. Trails close quickly when wet. Always check conditions that morning.
  • Bring layers for evening — coastal breeze drops temperatures after sunset even in warm months.
Food & Drink
  • Focaccia — the Ligurian staple. Eat it for breakfast. Eat it walking. Eat it often.
  • Trofie al pesto — fresh pasta with proper Ligurian basil pesto. Do not eat pesto from a jar for the rest of the year.
  • Anchovies (acciughe) from Monterosso are celebrated — marinated, grilled, or fried. Order them everywhere.
  • Sciacchetrà — the local dessert wine, amber and sweet, made from partially dried grapes. One small glass with dessert.
  • Avoid restaurants on the main piazzas with picture menus. Walk one street back and the food improves immediately.
