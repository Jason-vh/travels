Monterosso al Mare
The biggest village. Proper sandy beaches, grand hotels, and the famous giant Neptune statue. Best for a lazy beach afternoon.
⛵
02
Vernazza
Many people's favourite — a tiny natural harbour, colorful tower, and gelato eaten on the sea wall. Postcard perfection.
🌿
03
Corniglia
The only village with no direct sea access — perched 100m up on a cliff. Quietest, fewest tourists, great views. Worth the stairs.
🍷
04
Manarola
Wine country's edge — the vineyards almost tumble into the sea. Famous for the Sciacchetrà dessert wine and legendary sunset views.
🚪
05
Riomaggiore
Southern gateway to the park. Vibrant, a little grittier, great seafood trattorias tucked into the narrow main street.

📋 Know Before You Go

Practical Info

Everything you need to know so you can just enjoy the lemons.

🎟️

Cinque Terre Card

  • Required for hiking on the coastal trails (Sentiero Azzurro)
  • 1-day: ~€7.50 / 2-day: ~€14.50 per person
  • Includes trail access + some include train travel
  • Buy online in advance at parconazionale5terre.it or at station offices
💡 Get the Trekking + Train combo card for Day 1 — saves time and money vs. buying separately.
🚆

Getting Around by Train

  • Regional trains connect all 5 villages — runs every 15–30 min
  • Journey between villages: 3–8 minutes each!
  • Buy tickets at station machines or Trenitalia app
  • Validate tickets before boarding (yellow machines)
  • From La Spezia: direct trains to all villages ~20–40 min
⚠️ Trains get packed in summer — travel early or late to avoid crowds.
🌤️

Weather in May

  • Average highs: 20–24°C (68–75°F)
  • Sea temperature: ~18°C — swimable for the brave!
  • Morning mist is common, clears by midday
  • Occasional thunderstorms — pack a light rain layer
  • Sunscreen is essential — reflected heat off sea + stone
🍽️

Food & Drink Must-Haves

  • Trofie al pesto — the local pasta, made fresh
  • Focaccia al formaggio — especially in Recco nearby
  • Acciughe — anchovies, cured with salt and lemon
  • Farinata — crispy chickpea pancake, eaten standing up
  • Sciacchetrà — sweet dessert wine from local grapes
  • Limoncino — local lemon liqueur (not limoncello!)
👟

What to Pack

  • Comfortable hiking shoes (trail is rocky)
  • Swimsuit + quick-dry towel
  • Water shoes for rocky beaches
  • 2L water bottle minimum per person
  • Light rain jacket / windbreaker
  • Sunscreen, hat, sunglasses
  • Cash — many small places are cash only
  • Lightweight day bag / backpack
💰

Money & Budget Tips

  • ATMs in Monterosso, Vernazza, Riomaggiore
  • Budget €30–50/person/day for food + drinks
  • Tourist tax applies in all villages (~€2–4/night)
  • Ferries cost ~€6–8 per leg (worth it!)
  • Avoid peak lunchtime restaurants — prices jump
🧾 Eat where the menus are handwritten in Italian. That's always the right choice.
