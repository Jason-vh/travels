The Five Villages

A String of Cliff-Hung Jewels

Strung along 12 km of rugged Ligurian coastline, each village is its own world — connected by train, trail, and the deep blue of the Ligurian Sea.

I

Monterosso al Mare

The Beach Town

The largest and most relaxed village, with the only real sandy beach in Cinque Terre. Lemon groves, anchovies, and a laid-back resort energy.

II

Vernazza

The Postcard

Often called the most beautiful village. A tiny natural harbour ringed by colourful houses, a medieval watchtower, and gelato at every turn.

III

Corniglia

The Hilltop

The only village with no sea access — perched 100 m above the water. Quieter, cooler, and reached by 382 steps (or a shuttle). Worth every one.

IV

Manarola

The Romantic

Famous for its wine (sciacchetrà), rock-jumping cove, and the most photographed sunset view in all of Cinque Terre. Arrive at golden hour.

V

Riomaggiore

The Gateway

The southernmost village and natural entry point. A steep, colourful main street tumbles down to a small marina where the rocks are perfect for swimming.

Day by Day

The Itinerary

Four days shaped by sea air, footpaths, and unhurried meals that stretch long into the evening.

0 Arrival

Landing in Liguria

Friday · 22 May
Afternoon ✈️
Arrive & transfer — Fly into Genova Cristoforo Colombo (GOA) or La Spezia Centrale. The Cinque Terre train line begins at La Spezia. Tip: book a Frecciarossa from Milan if flying into MXP or LIN — around 2.5 hrs.
Evening 🏡
Check in & settle — Drop bags at accommodation, explore the village on foot, breathe the salt air.
Dinner 🍷
First dinner together — Find a terrace with sea views. Order focaccia, fresh anchovies, and a local Vermentino to kick things off right.
💡 Pick up your Cinque Terre Card at any train station — it covers unlimited trains between all five villages plus park entrance.
1 Hiking

The Northern Villages & High Trail

Saturday · 23 May
Morning ☀️
Early train to Monterosso — Start at the northernmost village while it's still cool. Grab a cornetto at a bar facing the sea.
Mid-morning 🥾
Sentiero Azzurro: Monterosso → Vernazza — The classic coastal trail (~2 hrs, moderate). Stunning clifftop views, vineyards terraced into the hillside, wildflowers in May. Trail: ~3.5 km, 200 m elevation gain. Wear proper shoes and bring water.
Lunch 🦐
Vernazza harbour lunch — Arrive into Vernazza's postcard harbour. Lunch at a spot overlooking the water — fresh pasta al pesto, stuffed mussels, a cold Pigato.
Afternoon 🏔️
Vernazza → Corniglia by trail or train — Either hike the second leg (~1.5 hrs) or take the short train. Climb the 382 steps (the Lardarina) up to Corniglia for rooftop views.
Late afternoon 🍦
Gelato & slow wander — Corniglia is tiny and quiet. Ice cream on the terrace, then the train back to base.
Evening 🍝
Dinner at base village — Trofie al pesto (the local pasta shape), grilled catch of the day, and local sciacchetrà dessert wine if you can find it.
🌡️ May highs average 18–22 °C — ideal hiking weather. Start early to beat the heat on the trail by 9 am.
2 Sea Day

Southern Villages & The Sea

Sunday · 24 May
Morning 🌊
Swimming at Riomaggiore — Take the train south to Riomaggiore. The marina rocks and small cove are perfect for a morning swim in the clearest water you've seen.
Mid-morning 🚂
Train to Manarola — Just one stop north. Stroll the narrow main street that drops to the sea, browse local wine shops.
Lunch 🍋
Manarola lunch & lazy afternoon — Eat on the rocks by the water. This is the day for doing nothing in particular: sun, sea, cold Spritz, good conversation. The Nessun Dorma bar has an iconic terrace view — worth the wait for a table.
Afternoon 🏊
Rock-jumping & swimming — Manarola's rocky cove is famous for cliff jumping. Optional — but spectacular for those who take the plunge.
Sunset 🌅
Golden hour at Manarola viewpoint — Walk up to the panoramic terrace above the village. This is the view — arguably the most photographed sunset in Italy. Be here for it.
Evening 🥂
Last full dinner together — Back at base for the final group dinner. Make it a proper celebration — the good wine, multiple courses, the works.
📸 For the iconic Manarola shot, walk up past the church to the Via dell'Amore viewpoint — arrive 30 min before sunset for position.
3 Departure

Slow Morning & Farewells

Monday · 25 May
Morning ☕
Last breakfast — No rushing. A long, slow espresso and pastry at a bar in the village. Let it linger.
Late morning 🧳
Check out & a final wander — One last walk to the waterfront before bags are collected. Stock up on pesto, anchovies, and olive oil to take home.
Midday 🚆
Train to La Spezia or Genova — Connect to intercity trains or airport transfers. La Spezia → Milan Centrale: ~2.5 hrs by Frecciarossa. La Spezia → Genova: ~1 hr.
🎒 Luggage storage is available at La Spezia Centrale station if you want a few extra hours to explore before flying out.

Need to Know

Practical Information

Everything to make the trip seamless — from train cards to table reservations.

🎫

Cinque Terre Card

  • 1-day card: €18 — unlimited trains + park access
  • 2-day card: €29 — best value for us (Days 1 & 2)
  • Covers all five villages on the local Trenitalia line
  • Also required to hike the Sentiero Azzurro (Blue Trail)
  • Buy at any of the five village train stations or online
Pro tip: The card does not cover intercity trains (La Spezia ↔ Genova). Buy separate tickets for those connections.
🚆

Getting Around

  • Trains run every 15–30 minutes between villages, all day
  • The journey between any two adjacent villages is just 2–5 minutes
  • Riomaggiore ↔ Monterosso (full line): ~12 minutes end to end
  • No cars within the villages — streets are pedestrian-only
  • Ferry boats run in summer (weather permitting) — scenic alternative
  • La Spezia is the main transport hub for intercity connections
🌤️

Weather in May

  • Average high: 18–22 °C — warm but not scorching
  • Sea temperature around 17–19 °C — refreshing for swimming
  • May has some rain days — pack a light layer & small umbrella
  • Golden hour light in May is spectacular — long evenings
  • Crowds build through the month but pre-summer is still manageable
What to pack: Sunscreen, a light waterproof layer, proper walking shoes with grip (the trails are rocky), and something nice for dinner.
🍽️

Food & Drink

  • Trofie al pesto genovese — the signature dish, mandatory
  • Acciughe (anchovies) — fresh, salted, fried — Monterosso is famous for them
  • Focaccia — buy from bakeries for the trail; better than anything you've had before
  • Farinata — crispy chickpea flatbread, eaten standing up
  • Sciacchetrà — local sweet dessert wine, produced on the terraces above the villages
  • Vermentino & Pigato — crisp Ligurian whites, perfect with seafood
🥾

The Trails

  • Sentiero Azzurro (Blue Trail) — the famous coastal path linking all 5 villages; sections vary in difficulty
  • Monterosso → Vernazza: ~2 hrs, moderate, most dramatic views
  • Vernazza → Corniglia: ~1.5 hrs, moderate, quieter
  • Corniglia → Manarola: ~2 hrs, strenuous in parts
  • Manarola → Riomaggiore (Via dell'Amore): currently under restoration — check status before trip
  • All trails require the Cinque Terre Card to access
💡

Insider Tips

  • Arrive at villages before 10 am or after 5 pm to avoid the worst cruise-ship day crowds
  • Bring cash — many small bars and food stalls don't take cards
  • The villages have very limited ATMs — stock up in La Spezia
  • Book popular restaurants a few days ahead, especially for weekends
  • Luggage storage in La Spezia Centrale: useful for late departures
  • Mobile signal is patchy on the trails — download offline maps (Maps.me or AllTrails)
