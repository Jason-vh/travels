Hike: Monterosso → Vernazza

The most iconic trail in the Cinque Terre. About 2 hours through terraced vineyards with jaw-dropping coastal views around every bend. Moderate difficulty — bring water!

Good to Know

Practical stuff

The things that'll save you time, money, and confusion.

🚆

Cinque Terre Express

A local train runs between all 5 villages every 15-20 min. Journey between any two villages: 2-8 minutes. Get the Cinque Terre Card (€16/day) for unlimited rides + trail access.

🥾

Hiking Trails

The Blue Trail (SVA) connects all 5 villages. Best section: Monterosso → Vernazza (~2h). Check trail status before going — some sections close seasonally. Included with the Cinque Terre Card.

💰

Cash & Cards

Bring some cash — smaller restaurants and shops may not take cards. ATMs exist in each village but can have queues. Budget ~€60-80/person/day for food & drinks.

👟

What to Wear

Good walking shoes are essential (cobblestones + trails). Swimsuit under clothes for spontaneous dips. Layers for evening — it can cool down by the sea.

🌡️

May Weather

Late May is ideal — 20-24°C, mostly sunny, not yet peak summer crowds. Sea temp around 18-20°C — refreshing but swimmable!
