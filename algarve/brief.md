## Base: Olhão

A working fishing town on the east Algarve that's been rediscovered over the last decade. Twin red-brick covered markets on the waterfront (the Saturday market is the best in the region), a strong cafe and restaurant scene, "cubist" whitewashed houses often converted into Airbnbs with rooftop terraces. More authentic and food-forward than the central Algarve, less polished than Tavira.

- **Beach access:** 15-min ferry from the waterfront to **Armona** or **Culatra** — wild Ria Formosa barrier-island beaches.
- **Sea view = lagoon view:** the Ria Formosa, not open Atlantic cliffs. Different aesthetic — calmer water, fishing boats, sometimes flamingos.
- **Walkability:** restaurants, market, waterfront, ferry terminal all on foot.
- **From Faro Airport:** ~10 km / 15 min — the shortest transfer of any Algarve base.

## Things to Do — in & around Olhão

A menu of options, not an itinerary. Headline picks marked ★.

### On foot from the base
- ★ **The twin markets** (daily; Saturday is peak) — fish in one building, fruit & veg in the other, stalls spilling out front. Go early.
- **Waterfront stroll** along the Jardim Patrão Joaquim Lopes — palm-lined, ferries arriving and departing, evening drinks spot.
- **Wandering the cubist old town** (Barreta and Levante neighbourhoods) — narrow whitewashed lanes, tiled doorways, rooftop terraces.
- **Sunset from the villa rooftop** — the cubist architecture is built for this; many Olhão Airbnbs have private terraces.

### Out on the Ria Formosa lagoon
- ★ **Ilha da Armona** — 15-min ferry, classic Algarve beach on the ocean side, mostly empty even in June. Cafe and a few restaurants. The default beach day.
- ★ **Ilha da Culatra + Farol** — 30–45 min ferry, small fishing village to wander, then a short walk across to Praia do Farol with its lighthouse. Long, wild beach.
- **Ilha Deserta (Barreta)** — uninhabited island. Lunch at **Estaminé** (one of the only buildings, sustainable seafood — books up).
- **Ria Formosa boat tour** — flat-water lagoon tour through mudflats and channels. Flamingos, spoonbills, fishermen on stilts.
- **Kayak / SUP** through lagoon channels — operators on the Olhão waterfront.
- **Oyster tasting on a boat** — local operators take you out to working oyster beds, opened straight from the water with a glass of wine.
- **Quinta de Marim** — Ria Formosa visitor centre with marsh boardwalks, an old tide mill, salt pans. Low-key half-morning.

### Ferry schedule (June)
- **Olhão → Armona** (15–20 min): 07:30, 09:30, 11:30, 12:30, 14:30, 15:30, 17:30, 18:30, 19:45. Return runs from Armona ~20 min after each.
- **Olhão → Culatra → Farol** (30–45 min): 07:00, 09:00, 11:00, 15:00, 17:15, 19:30.
- **Tickets:** adult return €4.20 (Armona/Culatra), €4.80 (Farol). **Cash only.** Buy at the terminal ticket office, opens 15–30 min before departure.

## The Dive (Jason & Victor)

Discovery dive — shallow reef (~12 m), half-morning slot, no certification needed. Briefing, kit, shallow-water skills, one boat dive, debrief. ~**€125 pp** in 2026 (gear included).

### Operators near Olhão
- **Tuna Dive Tours** (Olhão) — closest to the base, walk from the villa. Worth contacting for discovery-dive availability and pricing.
- **Hidroespaço** (Faro) — ~15 min drive west. Long-established, strong reviews, recommended for first-timers.
- **Openwaters Diving Center** (Quarteira) — ~30 min west. Another solid PADI option.
- **Algarve Divexperience** (Carvoeiro) — ~50 min west. 5-star PADI Resort that explicitly markets discovery dives to first-timers. Combines naturally with a cliff-coast day trip.

### Practical
- Book a week+ ahead for late June
- Half-morning commitment (~08:00–12:30)
- Plan a chill afternoon after — fatigue is real on a first dive
- **Don't pair with a Monchique day** (altitude rule). Coastal driving is fine.
- Don't dive on the day of the return flight (12-hr no-fly rule)

## Day Trips Further Afield

### Cliff-coast day: Carvoeiro / Benagil / Marinha (~1h drive west) ★
The "iconic Algarve" day you don't get from Olhão. Combine:
- **Sete Vales Suspensos** clifftop walk (6 km, Carvoeiro → Praia da Marinha) — do it before 10am in June to beat the heat
- **Benagil sea cave** by boat, kayak, or SUP. **2026 rules to know:** guided tours only (no independent rentals to the cave), no swimming in, no disembarking on the beach inside, 5–10 min on a boat / 8 min on kayak/SUP. Kayak tours ~€35–50 pp, 2–3 hours total.
- **Praia da Marinha** for the afternoon

### Seville, Spain (~2h drive east) ★
Genuinely doable as a day trip from Olhão (which wouldn't be possible from Lagos). Tapas, alcázar, cathedral, Plaza de España. One full-on day but memorable.

### Sagres + Cape St. Vincent (~2h drive west)
Mainland Europe's southwestern tip. Dramatic cliffs, surfer beaches (Praia do Amado, Bordeira), fortress, sunset at the cape. Long day but high-impact.

### Monchique mountains (~1h45 inland)
Cool air, thermal springs at Caldas de Monchique, sweeping coast views from Fóia. Good antidote to beach saturation. *Not on the dive day — altitude rule applies.*

### Closer-in east-Algarve outings (~30–45 min)
- ★ **Tavira town** — climb the castle ruins, walk the cobbles, lunch by the river. Prettiest town in the region.
- **Santa Luzia** — octopus capital. Lunch at one of the polvo spots (Casa do Polvo Tasquinha is the classic).
- **Cabanas** — pretty waterfront village with a boardwalk along the lagoon.
- **Loulé Saturday market** — bigger and more handicrafts-y than Olhão's, ~30 min north.
- **Estoi** — small village with a pink rococo palace + Roman ruins of Milreu, ~25 min.
- **Faro old town** — walled cidade velha, often skipped but lovely for a couple of hours.
- **Silves** (~1h west) — Moorish red-stone castle in a medieval town. Pairs with Monchique or Carvoeiro/Benagil.
